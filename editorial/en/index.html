Travel guide editorial policy

Mangen travel guides are written in Chinese. They explain routes, transport, and on-site rules for a trip in Japan. This page describes how those guides are produced. It does not replace the source links inside each article.

Research

Guides are compiled from material published by transport operators, venues, local governments, and event organizers. Links to those official sources stay in the article. A guide is not a live lookup for tickets, weather, or autumn leaves.

Artificial intelligence

AI may assist with an outline or a draft. When an image is a diagram, it keeps the existing disclosure: 「示意图（AI 辅助制作），非实景，仅供参考。」 AI does not replace checking a fact against the official source.

Fact checking

Before publication, dates, transport, opening information, and on-site rules are checked against the official sources cited in the article. When a guide has a review date, the same note appears at the end: 资料核对：日期（日本时间）。营业、交通、商品及现场规则可能调整，出行前请再核对文中官方来源。

Reviewer

Reviewed by the Editorial Team.

Corrections

If a date, fare, transport detail, or source is wrong, email info@mangen.jp or use Contact. Include the article title and the sentence to change. The correction is checked against the official source. The page shows an updated date only when the article content actually changes.
